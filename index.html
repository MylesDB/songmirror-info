SongMirror keeps selected Spotify playlists up to date in YouTube Music. This site describes MylesDB’s private, personal installation.

Spotify → YouTube Music

What the app does

The installation reads selected Spotify playlist information, finds corresponding tracks on YouTube Music, and creates or updates destination playlists. Syncs can be started manually or scheduled. Spotify is the source for this one-way setup.

Why it connects to Google

Google authorization allows the private app to access the selected YouTube identity’s playlist and library information and make playlist changes needed for synchronization. The account owner chooses the identity and authorizes access through Google.

A private app, a public information page

The app runs on the owner’s server. This website provides app information and a privacy policy; it offers no sign-in, account connection or public sync service.

Questions about this installation or its data handling can be raised through this site’s GitHub issues. Please do not post passwords, tokens or private playlist information.
